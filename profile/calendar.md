# Annual calendar

Things that come around every year and belong in the email when their season arrives. Search for the current year's dates and details; never assume last year's schedule.

## September
- Oktoberfest season starts (German beer halls and gardens, Bierhaus, Radegast, Zum Stammtisch, Deutsches Haus programming). Look for the ones that are actually German, not frat parties.
- UFC and boxing fall cards get announced; Nutcracker tickets typically go on sale for New York City Ballet.
- Last beach days: Jacob Riis, Rockaway, Fort Tilden. Last outdoor day parties.
- Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur fall here or in October; Jewish cultural programming ramps up.
- German Unity Day is October 3; German cultural events cluster around it.

## October
- Halloween: Company XIV's Halloween show, Rocky Horror screenings with costumes, costume dance nights, drag shows, the Village Halloween Parade (skip the parade itself, but the parties around it count).
- Fall fight cards at MSG and Barclays.
- Open House New York weekend (access to buildings you normally can't enter).
- NYC Marathon is early November; the week before is when running-related events happen.
- Sukkot and Simchat Torah events.

## November
- Nutcracker at Lincoln Center opens late November (New York City Ballet). Book early.
- St. Martin's Day lantern walks (Laternenumzug) in the German community, typically around November 11. A perfect family pick.
- Brooklyn Botanic Garden Lightscape opens.
- Thanksgiving week: Friendsgiving-style group dinners, the night-before-Thanksgiving bar night.
- Knicks season is underway; big rivalry games.
- Hanukkah can start in late November or December: the Hanukkah pop-up cocktail bar (Maccabee Bar) returns.

## December
- Fort Greene Park tree lighting (early December). Automatic pick.
- Dyker Heights Christmas lights. Automatic pick, ideally with friends and a thermos.
- German Christmas markets, Bryant Park's market is the obvious one; find the less obvious ones.
- Hanukkah bar, Jewish holiday programming, Christmas Eve Chinese food traditions.
- Nutcracker runs all month.
- New Year's Eve: the good warehouse parties sell out early; put them on the radar in November.

## January
- Deep winter: bathhouse and sauna season. Russian and Turkish Baths, World Spa, Bathhouse, banya days.
- Winter Jazzfest, Under the Radar theater festival.
- UFC and boxing January cards.
- Lunar New Year (late January or February): Chinatown and Sunset Park events, banquet dinners.

## February
- Super Bowl watch parties with the guys.
- Valentine's counter-programming: burlesque, Company XIV's Valentine's run, a serious tasting menu.
- Berlinale season in Berlin; Goethe-Institut and German film programming in New York.
- Knicks trade deadline energy, big February games.
- Purim parties (late February or March).

## March
- St. Patrick's Day: skip the obvious, find the Irish music session in a real pub.
- UFC and boxing spring cards; March Madness watch parties.
- Passover (March or April): Seders, Passover-themed dinners.
- Spring day parties start to get announced.

## April
- Knicks playoffs begin: watch parties in bars with the guys are automatic during a run.
- Outdoor season opens: The Lot Radio, rooftop and pier sets, brewery yards.
- Tribeca Festival announcements; Frieze week art parties in May.
- Spring German events, Easter markets.

## May
- Knicks playoffs continue. Frieze New York art fair week parties.
- Governors Island opens fully; QC NY spa on the island.
- Outdoor day parties every weekend. Brooklyn Mirage and outdoor venues open.
- Memorial Day weekend: beach season begins at Riis.
- Bike Month: group rides, Five Boro Bike Tour.

## June
- Governors Ball, Pride month parties (drag shows and the good parties, not the corporate float).
- Summer fight cards. Rooftop pool day passes (TWA Hotel pool opens).
- Make Music New York (June 21): free live music on every block.
- Summer Streets planning; outdoor movies begin.
- Tony Awards, if the wife cares.

## July
- Fourth of July: rooftops, boats, the Coney Island hot dog contest with friends as a bit.
- Peak beach season: Jacob Riis, Fort Tilden, Rockaway surf.
- Day parties, boat parties, pier sets. Summer Streets in August; look for July announcements.
- Outdoor sauna and cold plunge events by the water.

## August
- Summer Streets (car-free Manhattan avenues, great for the bike crew).
- US Open tennis starts late August; night sessions with friends.
- Labor Day weekend: J'Ouvert and the West Indian Day Parade in Crown Heights (Caribbean Brooklyn's biggest weekend, directly relevant to his Afrobeats and dancehall taste).
- Afropunk if it returns. Last of the beach days and boat parties.
- Fall show announcements begin: put September and October big shows on the radar.
