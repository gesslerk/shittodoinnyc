# Music profile

Derived from Konrad's full Spotify liked-songs library (685 tracks, 2016 to September 2026). Use this to judge whether a show, DJ, party or dance night fits. Recency matters: what he added in the last few months is what he is in the mood for.

## Tier 1: the core

**Melodic, progressive house and melodic techno.** deadmau5 is the single most-liked artist by a wide margin (Strobe-era through the 2025 and 2026 singles: I Remember, HR 8938 Cephei, Faxing Berlin, The Veldt, Brazil, Jaded, Ameonna, Patience, COASTED, Science, Not Exactly). Around him: Kx5 (deadmau5 and Kaskade), Eric Prydz and Pryda, Paul Kalkbrenner (Berlin Calling), Rebūke, Tinlicker, Marsh, Nu Aspect, Fred again.., John Summit and HAYLA, Disclosure, Galantis, Kaskade, Morgan Page, Medina remixes, Anjunadeep-style melodic sets. Daft Punk is a touchstone. Also a live-band-electronic streak (Polo & Pan, Empire of the Sun, Washed Out, Djo).

What this means for picks: big-name melodic house and techno bookings at Knockdown Center, Avant Gardner and the Mirage, the Great Hall, Brooklyn Storehouse, Under the K Bridge, Brooklyn Steel; serious smaller rooms like Public Records, Nowadays, Good Room, Basement, Elsewhere; day parties and The Lot Radio-style sets; Berlin techno nights; warehouse promoters like Teksupport and Zero. The Kx5 "We Belong Here" show is his reference point for a great big night.

**Afrobeats, Afro-fusion, dancehall and amapiano.** The most recent adds are all this: Burna Boy, Wizkid and Tems, Fireboy DML, MOLIY and Shenseea. Deeper: Rema, Tyla, Omah Lay, CKay, Victony, 1da Banton, Ayo Jay, Maleek Berry, Korede Bello, Master KG. Dancehall and reggae: Sean Paul, Shaggy, Popcaan, Gyptian, Charly Black, Alkaline, Seeed. This is the current phase. Afrobeats parties, amapiano nights, dancehall parties in Flatbush and Crown Heights, big Afrobeats arena shows, and Caribbean Brooklyn events are all strong.

**Hip-hop and trap, across the whole timeline.** Drake and Travis Scott dominate the library. Then Don Toliver, Gunna, Lil Baby, Young Thug, Future, Metro Boomin, Roddy Ricch, Juice WRLD, Lil Uzi Vert, Playboi Carti, Lil Tecca, Jack Harlow, Post Malone, Kanye West, Kendrick Lamar, A$AP Rocky, Cardi B, Nicki Minaj, Ice Spice, Migos, Rae Sremmurd, DaBaby, NLE Choppa, Kodak Black, 21 Savage, Pop Smoke and Fivio Foreign (Brooklyn drill), Lil Peep. A 2000s New York rap streak: Cam'ron and Juelz Santana, Styles P, Lloyd Banks, Big Pun, T.I., Ludacris, Twista. Hip-hop shows, 2000s New York rap nights, and Brooklyn drill moments all fit.

## How to use this

- Melodic house and techno, Afrobeats and dancehall, and hip-hop are the three lanes to search hardest every week.
- German artists, Berlin techno, Latin nights, emo or 2000s nights, ABBA or disco nights, and classical spectacle are the "once in a while, and he'll love it" lanes.
- He does not need the biggest name. He needs the right room and the right sound. A great DJ in a 150-person room beats a mediocre arena show.
- When a Tier 1 artist plays a big NYC show, it belongs in the book-now radar as soon as tickets go on sale.
