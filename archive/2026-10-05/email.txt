SHIT TO DO IN NYC  ·  No. 04  ·  Week of Oct 5, 2026
============================================================

Three-day weekend, and the city noticed: Knockdown goes fourteen hours, Good Room gets the only US stop of RA's anniversary tour, and Sunday is a free day because Monday is a holiday. Pick your damage early, because the good rooms this week are small.

THIS WEEK
------------------------------------------------------------
01. Honey Dijon takes Knockdown, 3pm to 5am  [the guys, music]
    Sat Oct 10, 3pm to 5am; starts outdoors in The Ruins, ends in the Main Hall  ·  Knockdown Center, Maspeth
    This is the day-party-into-warehouse format you already know you love, except the person behind the decks spent thirty years actually building house music. Start outside in The Ruins at four with daylight and a beer, let it move inside, leave whenever your body says so. Derrick Carter and Luke Solomon are the support, which is absurd for a Saturday afternoon.
    Heads up: 21+, tickets on RA and DICE, and RA isn't listing a price. Fourteen hours is a trap; go early for The Ruins and treat the Main Hall close as optional.
    Text to send: "Honey Dijon is doing fourteen hours at Knockdown on Saturday starting at 3. I'm going for the outdoor half around 4, come find me."
    https://ra.co/events/2513628

02. RA's 25th tour, one night, Good Room  [the guys, music]
    Fri Oct 9, night, both floors  ·  Good Room, Greenpoint
    Ten dates worldwide and New York gets one, in a 600-cap Greenpoint club instead of a shed. Nick León headlining with Galcher Lustwerk and Stacey Hotwaxx Hale b2b Kilopatrah Jones is a lineup nobody outside the scene is tracking. Both floors, Friday, and you can see everyone's face.
    Heads up: Tickets through RA and there's still one unannounced name on the bill. This is the kind of one-stop booking that goes quietly and then is gone.
    Text to send: "Resident Advisor's 25th anniversary tour has exactly one New York night and it's Friday at Good Room. Nick León headlining, both floors. In?"
    https://ra.co/news/86003

03. ZickZack, the German punk label, on Essex Street  [the guys, culture]
    opening reception Thu Oct 8, 6-8pm; gallery hours Thursday-Sunday 3-8pm  ·  Various/Artists, Lower East Side
    First show ever mounted outside Germany about Alfred Hilsberg and ZickZack, the Hamburg label that put out Einstürzende Neubauten and basically invented the Neue Deutsche Welle underground. Rare records, flyers, badges, Super-8. It's a gallery you could cross in ten steps, run by the Goethe-Institut with no marketing budget, which is the exact register you keep asking for.
    Heads up: Opening reception is Thursday 6 to 8pm. If Thursday is spoken for, the show runs through Nov 7 with gallery hours Thursday to Sunday, 3 to 8pm. The 6pm event on the 8th is an opening reception, not a talk, and after that the gallery is only open Thursday to Sunday, 3-8pm.
    Text to send: "Thursday there's an opening on the Lower East Side for the German punk label that put out Einstürzende Neubauten. Rare records and flyers, tiny room, 6 to 8. Drinks after."
    https://www.goethe.de/ins/us/en/index.html

04. Balanchine and Robbins, Koch Theater, two nights  [date night, performance]
    Fri Oct 9 to Sat Oct 10, evening performances both nights, about 2 hours 10 minutes  ·  David H. Koch Theater, Lincoln Center
    The Nutcracker at Lincoln Center is still your favorite night in the city, and this is the same company in the same house without waiting until December. Three works, pianist onstage, about two hours ten. The Concert is Robbins being genuinely funny, so she isn't sitting through an hour of abstraction to get to the good part.
    Heads up: Friday and Saturday evenings only, and these are the last two outings before the fall season closes Oct 18. Book the sitter first, tickets second.
    Text to send: "Got an idea for Friday: City Ballet at the Koch, three Balanchine and Robbins pieces, ends early enough for a drink after. Want me to get the sitter?"
    https://www.nycballet.com/season-and-tickets/fall-2026/masters-at-work-i

05. The George Lucas Talk Show at Littlefield  [the guys, comedy]
    Thu Oct 8, doors 7pm, show 8pm  ·  Littlefield, Gowanus
    Connor Ratliff interviews real guests in character as a retired George Lucas, with a sidekick named Watto, and has been doing it long enough that it's become its own small institution. Cult comedy with a committed bit beats a club with a two-drink minimum every time. Gowanus, Thursday, doors at seven.
    Heads up: 21+, show at 8pm, and the guests get announced close to showtime, so book on faith. Parklife is right there afterward.
    Text to send: "Thursday: the George Lucas Talk Show at Littlefield, guy does a whole fake George Lucas late-night thing with real guests. Doors at 7, drinks after."
    https://littlefieldnyc.com/event/?wfea_eb_id=1999959468608

06. A RÜFÜS DU SOL night inside a sauna  [the guys, wellness]
    Fri Oct 9, 9pm to 11:30pm  ·  Othership Flatiron, Flatiron
    One night, one catalog, the entire Othership sound system given over to RÜFÜS DU SOL start to finish. Melodic house with real low end is your core lane, and this plays it at 185 degrees with ice baths between rounds instead of a club floor. Friday night out, no bar, home at a reasonable hour.
    Heads up: 9pm to 11:30pm, booked as a timed session so capacity is tiny and it sells out. Flatiron location, not Williamsburg.
    Text to send: "Friday night: Othership is playing nothing but RÜFÜS DU SOL through the sauna for two and a half hours, ice baths in between. Weird and good. Booking two spots."
    https://www.othership.us/events/rufus-du-sol-listening-night-new-york

07. Roman Flügel in the Public Records nursery  [guys or date, music]
    Sun Oct 11, 3pm, afternoon into evening  ·  Public Records, Gowanus
    Public Records opens the outdoor nursery on Sunday afternoon for Frank & Tony and Roman Flügel, a Frankfurt lifer with thirty years of records behind him. Best sound in Brooklyn, fifteen minutes from your door, outdoors in October while that's still possible. Holiday Monday means a 3pm start doesn't cost you anything.
    Heads up: Outdoors, so dress for a Gowanus afternoon turning cold. Tickets via DICE; it's a small space and the long-weekend Sunday crowd will find it.
    Text to send: "Sunday afternoon at Public Records, outdoor nursery, Roman Flügel playing from 3. Holiday Monday so there's no reason not to. Come through."
    https://link.dice.fm/P1b111e64273

ANYTIME

Limousine in Greenpoint has a private karaoke room  [the guys, bar]
    small bites until midnight  ·  Limousine, Greenpoint
    From the Five Leaves and Goldie's team, in the old Ponyboy space: black banquettes, a real cocktail list, snacks until midnight, and a karaoke room you can book. That's a plan, not a 'grab a drink' text.
    https://www.theinfatuation.com/new-york/guides/new-nyc-restaurants-openings

Cmarty's jerk pop-up inside King Tai  [the guys, food]
    Wednesdays 5-11pm, Thursdays and Fridays 5-9pm  ·  King Tai, Crown Heights
    A Jamaican-American jerk kitchen running out of a small Crown Heights bar: jerk banh mi, bourbon wings, pork shu mai, menu changes on the cook's whim. Restaurant-inside-a-bar, walkable-ish from Fort Greene, Wednesday through Friday evenings.
    Heads up: Cmarty's is only inside King Tai Wednesday through Friday evenings, not earlier in the week.
    https://www.theinfatuation.com/new-york/reviews/cmartys-jerk

The Tiny Cupboard, stand-up every night in Bushwick  [the guys, comedy]
    shows nightly; Sun–Thu typically 8pm & 9:30pm (plus 11pm open mic), Fri/Sat 7pm, 8:30pm & 10:15pm  ·  The Tiny Cupboard, Bushwick  ·  $13.50 + $2.41 fee online Sun–Thu ($20 at the door); Fri/Sat shows $19.50 + $2.82 fee online, $25 at the door
    A rooftop room and a small indoor room, shows every single night, thirteen bucks online and no drink minimum. There's a board game and video game bar out back so the night keeps going without a second venue. This is your 48-hours-notice answer in Bushwick.
    Heads up: Weeknight shows are the $13.50 ones; Friday and Saturday shows run $19.50 online and $25 at the door.
    https://www.thetinycupboard.com/calendar
