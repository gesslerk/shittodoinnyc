SHIT TO DO IN NYC  ·  No. 07  ·  Week of Oct 26, 2026
============================================================

Halloween lands on a Saturday, which means the whole city is doing something and most of it is bad. Here are the rooms worth being in: amapiano in Bushwick, a record shop's costume ball in the Public Records sound room, a live-band Rocky Horror in Gowanus, and a warehouse on Sunday that's done by midnight. Pick one Friday, one Saturday, and keep Sunday clear.

THIS WEEK
------------------------------------------------------------
01. Major League DJz, Halloween at Elsewhere  [the guys, music]
    Sat Oct 31, doors 10:30pm, runs to 4am  ·  Elsewhere, Bushwick  ·  from $35
    Amapiano is the phase you're in and Major League DJz are the people who exported it. Elsewhere hands over the entire building, so you get the Hall, Zone One and the Loft for one ticket. Costume night, Bushwick, and none of the Manhattan Afrobeats-with-a-table-minimum nonsense.

02. Dope Jams Halloween Ball, Public Records  [the guys, party]
    Sat Oct 31, 11pm until late  ·  Public Records, Gowanus
    A record shop's annual costume party in the Public Records Sound Room, which holds about 150 and is built for hi-fi. Turtle Bugg, 4AM NYC and GE-OLOGY playing actual records to people who care about them. This never makes a list, which is exactly the point, and it's fifteen minutes from your door.
    Heads up: Doors around 11pm, price not posted. Costume is the premise, not a suggestion. Could not re-check the listing page this morning. Confirm the date on the link before you buy.

03. Rocky Horror with a live band, Littlefield  [the guys, performance]
    Fri Oct 30, doors 7pm, show 7:30pm  ·  Littlefield, Gowanus
    You dressed up for Rocky Horror in Flatbush and had a great night. This is the upgrade: The Occasionalists play the whole score live while 300 people in Gowanus sing and heckle. Twelfth year running, doors at 7, which means you're out early enough to do something after.
    Heads up: Friday, doors 7pm, show 7:30pm, 21+. It's an annual sellout — book before Wednesday. Costume is not optional if you want to enjoy yourself.
    Text to send: "Friday: Rocky Horror at Littlefield with a live band playing the score, 7:30. We dress up, we yell at the screen. Buying tickets tonight."
    https://littlefieldnyc.com/event/?wfea_eb_id=2000339654755

04. 2ManyDJs at Good Room, Thursday  [the guys, music]
    Thu Oct 29, 10pm to 4am  ·  Good Room, Greenpoint  ·  $30 to $40
    The Soulwax brothers doing the mashup set in a Greenpoint room that holds a few hundred, not a warehouse that holds five thousand. Expect Daft Punk-adjacent edits running into disco and house all night, and a sound system that deserves it. This is the Thursday you actually use.
    Heads up: $30 to $40 on RA, 10pm to 4am, 21+. It's a late one for a Thursday — set a hard exit at 1am or accept the consequences.
    Text to send: "2ManyDJs are at Good Room this Thursday, small room, 30 bucks. I'm going for a couple of hours, come for the first half at least."
    https://ra.co/events/2562732

05. Dirty Circus at House of Yes, seated  [date night, performance]
    Fri Oct 30 to Sat Oct 31, doors 6:30pm, show 7:15pm  ·  House of Yes, Bushwick

    Heads up: Halloween editions run both Friday and Saturday. Seating isn't guaranteed after 7:30pm, so get there by 7 or book a table outright.

06. Dixon and Jimi Jules, noon to midnight  [the guys, party]
    Sun Nov 1, noon to midnight  ·  Brooklyn Storehouse, Brooklyn Navy Yard  ·  splurge

    Heads up: Final tickets were being pushed on DICE and RA this week and resale was running near $200, so this is a genuine splurge. Confirm the Sunday session on the ticket page before you buy — the three nights get listed confusingly. 21+.

07. Red Hook's Barnacle Parade, Thursday  [guys or date, community]
    Thu Oct 29, evening, gathers around Van Brunt and Pioneer  ·  Red Hook streets, Red Hook  ·  free
    Twelve years of neighbors making homemade sea creatures and walking them through Red Hook on the Sandy anniversary. No institution behind it, no marketing, proceeds to Red Hook Mutual Aid. Strong Rope brewed the Barnacle King golden ale for it, which answers the question of where you go afterward.
    Heads up: Free, gathers around Van Brunt and Pioneer in the evening — exact step-off time isn't posted, so check their feed the day of. Dress for Red Hook wind. Could not re-check the listing page this morning. Confirm the date on the link before you buy.

ANYTIME

Othership Williamsburg, guided hot and cold  [guys or date, wellness]
    Recurring  ·  Othership Williamsburg, Williamsburg
    Guided rounds in a big cedar sauna with ice baths, which is the participatory version of a plunge instead of staring at a sand timer. It's a short walk from where you train, so it turns a Muay Thai night into an actual plan. Book class by class.
    https://www.othership.us/williamsburg

Governors Island, off-season and empty  [with the kid, family]
    daytime; ferry schedule varies by season  ·  Governors Island, Governors Island  ·  not verified for the current season
    The Pier 6 ferry from Brooklyn Bridge Park is the easy one, and November is the best version of the island: flat, nearly deserted, and the boat itself entertains a one-year-old for the whole ride. Check the current ferry schedule before you leave the house — I couldn't confirm it.
    https://www.govisland.com/
