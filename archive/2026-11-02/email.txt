SHIT TO DO IN NYC  ·  No. 08  ·  Week of Nov 2, 2026
============================================================

Four Germans, one Rotterdam label and the entire Hamburg punk archive all landed in New York the same week, which is lucky because the fight calendar is a TV card. Thursday and Friday are stacked, Sunday is natural wine early and drag queens versus Freddy Krueger late. Pick a lane.

THIS WEEK
------------------------------------------------------------
01. Mousse T. in the Public Records Atrium  [the guys, music]
    Thu Nov 5, 10:30pm start  ·  Public Records, Gowanus  ·  $32 to $37
    A German house lifer in a room that holds about 150 people, on your main weeknight. The Atrium sound system is the reason to go even when you don't know the DJ, and here you do. Fifteen minutes from your door, no stage, no spectacle, just a guy who has been making disco-house since before Hanover was cool.
    Heads up: $32 to $37 on Dice. The 10:30pm start makes it a genuinely late Thursday, so eat first and warn the morning shift.
    Text to send: "Mousse T is playing the little room at Public Records Thursday night, starts around 10:30. In or out?"
    https://link.dice.fm/t6a032c11e33

02. Brutalismus 3000 and SPFDJ at Knockdown  [the guys, music]
    Fri Nov 6, 8pm to 2am  ·  Knockdown Center, Maspeth  ·  resale listings around $65; face value was on Dice
    This is the Berlin night of the year so far: a German duo doing gabber-punk-techno live, with SPFDJ playing hard underneath them, in the big raw Maspeth room. Nothing about it is polite. If Kalkbrenner is the melodic end of your Berlin interest, this is the other end, and it's a Friday so you can take two or three of the guys.
    Heads up: Face value was on Dice; resale has been sitting around $65. Runs 8pm to 2am, and Knockdown is a cold walk from the train — dress for it. Knockdown Center's own listing says the night runs 8pm to 2am, not a 7pm start.
    Text to send: "Brutalismus 3000 are doing their live show at Knockdown on Friday with SPFDJ. Berlin techno in a warehouse, I'm grabbing tickets, say the word."
    https://www.bandsintown.com/e/108982102-brutalismus-3000-at-knockdown-center

03. Benji B, Sound Room, 11pm to 4am  [the guys, music]
    Sat Nov 7, 11pm to 4am  ·  Public Records, Gowanus  ·  splurge
    Benji B is one of the few DJs alive who can go from broken beat to house to hip-hop inside one set and make it make sense, which is roughly how your library reads. Sound Room holds about 200 people and the system is the best in Brooklyn. Three rooms running, so there's somewhere to stand when you need to hear yourself.
    Heads up: Tickets through RA; door sales depend on capacity. 21+, coats and bags must be checked, and doors close an hour before the 4am finish.
    Text to send: "Benji B is at Public Records Saturday, 11 til 4. Going for the first couple hours at least, come find me in the Sound Room."
    https://ra.co/events/2554762

04. Tokyo's Sushi Saito takes over Sushi Noz  [date night, food]
    Thu Nov 5 to Sat Nov 7, seatings at 6pm and 9pm each night  ·  Sushi Noz, Upper East Side  ·  $1,200 to $1,800 per person  ·  splurge
    Saito is one of the hardest reservations on earth and this is billed as his first US collaboration dinner, three nights only, at a counter that seats a handful of people. This is the definition of the thing you had to hear about from someone. Brutally expensive, and worth it as the one big date night of the season.
    Heads up: $1,200 to $1,800 a head, seatings at 6pm and 9pm, Thursday through Saturday only. Seats like this rarely appear on a public booking page, so call the restaurant directly. Saito's counter is $1,200-$1,800 per person, with seatings at 6pm and 9pm on Nov 5, 6 and 7.
    Text to send: "Sushi Saito from Tokyo is cooking at Sushi Noz Thursday through Saturday, first time he's done anything in the States. I'll call about a counter seat for two if you can get the sitter."
    https://www.timeout.com/newyork/news/a-tokyo-sushi-legend-is-bringing-his-impossible-to-book-omakase-to-nyc-for-3-nights-only-102026

05. ZickZack closes with a DJ on Essex  [guys or date, culture]
    Sat Nov 7, 6pm to 9pm  ·  Various/Artists (19 Essex), Lower East Side  ·  free
    The first show outside Germany about Alfred Hilsberg and the label that invented Neue Deutsche Welle: records, badges, flyers, Super-8 films, in a storefront the size of a living room. Closing reception with a DJ, which makes it an art opening that's actually a party, the register you liked at that photo studio. Free, 6 to 9, and you can be at dinner by 10.
    Heads up: Free with no ticket, 6pm to 9pm. It closes that night, so there is no next weekend on this one.
    Text to send: "There's a closing party Saturday evening for a German punk label exhibit on Essex Street, free, with a DJ. Drink after on Orchard?"
    https://www.variousartistsrecords.com/zickzack

06. RAW WINE lands at Industry City  [guys or date, drink]
    Sun Nov 8 to Mon Nov 9, Sun 12pm to 6pm, Mon 12pm to 5pm  ·  FIVE-TWO-A, Industry City, Sunset Park  ·  roughly $30 to $110 depending on ticket type
    Growers pouring their own bottles, a few hundred of them, organic and low-intervention and mostly imported by people with one van. It's a fair, not a party, which is the point. Sunday daytime in Sunset Park is your slot and it's the most civilized way to drink all weekend.
    Heads up: Sunday noon to 6pm is the public day; tickets run roughly $30 to $110 by tier. Buy ahead, and eat something first.
    Text to send: "RAW WINE is at Industry City Sunday afternoon, a few hundred natural wine growers pouring their own stuff. Want to go for a couple hours before dinner?"
    https://www.rawwine.com/fairs/new-york-2026

07. Drag queens and Freddy vs Jason, All Night Skate  [the guys, performance]
    Sun Nov 8, evening; check RSVP for start time  ·  All Night Skate, Bushwick
    The Nobodies pair drag performances with a screening of Freddy vs Jason in the back of a bar you already like, and everybody talks over the movie. Same register as the Rocky Horror night in Flatbush, minus the costume logistics. Sunday, cheap, two friends, done.
