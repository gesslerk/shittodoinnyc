SHIT TO DO IN NYC  ·  No. 06  ·  Week of Oct 19, 2026
============================================================

Halloween week is next week, which means this is the last one where you can do something that isn't in a costume. Use it. Friday is a warehouse, Saturday runs from SoHo cobblestones to a 5am close, and Sunday you're either on a bike by 7am or you're not.

THIS WEEK
------------------------------------------------------------
01. Massano takes Knockdown's main hall  [the guys, music]
    Fri Oct 23, doors 10pm, Massano late  ·  Knockdown Center, Maspeth  ·  $32 on Dice (all-in $43)
    This is the Afterlife-adjacent end of melodic techno, the Rebūke and Tinlicker lane you actually live in. Big industrial room, real sound, nobody selling you a table. Konstantin Sibold and SOEL open, Massano plays late.
    Heads up: $43 all-in on Dice and resale is flagging it as nearly gone, so buy before Wednesday. Doors 10pm, 21+, Maspeth, so plan the car home.
    Text to send: "Massano at Knockdown this Friday, melodic techno in the big room. Grabbing tickets tonight before it sells, you in?"
    https://dice.fm/event/pywyyy-massano-konstantin-sibold-soel-23rd-oct-knockdown-center-new-york-tickets

02. Witches and Stitches crowns a winner  [the guys, performance]
    Thu Oct 22, doors 8pm  ·  All Night Skate, Ocean Hill / Bed-Stuy  ·  $10 suggested donation
    A year-long drag and costume competition ending at All Night Skate, which you already love, on a Thursday, for ten bucks. Every monthly winner comes back to fight for it and the theme is urban legends. This is the night that bar's actual community shows up for, not a marketed drag brunch.
    Heads up: $10 suggested donation at the door, doors 8pm. Get there early, finale nights at that room fill up and seating is whatever you claim.
    Text to send: "Thursday: the drag costume competition at All Night Skate is doing its big year-end finale, ten bucks at the door. Doors at 8, I'm going."
    https://www.instagram.com/p/DeM1WqxIBv3/

03. NYC Century: 55 miles, four boroughs  [the guys, outdoors]
    Sun Oct 25, rolling start from 6am, event runs to 6pm  ·  NYC Century Bike Tour (Transportation Alternatives)  ·  $130 general, $205 VIP on Zeffy
    You rode to the GW Bridge with friends and talked about it for months. This is that ride with aid stations, 2,000 other people and a mileage option for whoever in the crew is soft. Take the 40 or 55 and you're showered and home by early afternoon.
    Heads up: $130 general, $205 VIP, register before Sunday. Rolling start from 6am and the event window runs to 6pm, so pick your route and start time now rather than at the line.
    Text to send: "Sunday is the NYC Century bike tour, 40 or 55 mile options, rolling start early morning. Signing up tonight, come do the 55 with me."
    https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/ticketing/nyc-century-bike-tour--2026

04. Company XIV's Petite Rouge, final two weeks  [date night, performance]
    Thu Oct 22 to Sat Oct 31, Thu, Fri and Sat at 8pm; Sun at 6pm  ·  Théâtre XIV, Bushwick  ·  from $35 Thursday, from $39 Sunday, from $49 Friday and Saturday
    Your favorite room in the city, and this one closes October 31 before they flip the theater over to the Nutcracker. Baroque burlesque Little Red Riding Hood, cocktails at the table, 21+. Sunday at 6pm is the easy babysitter math; Thursday at 8pm is the standard date night.
    Heads up: Seats start around $35 Thursday, $39 Sunday and $49 Friday and Saturday, with couch and champagne upgrades on top. Listings disagree slightly on which nights are dark, so confirm the curtain time on their calendar before you book the sitter.
    Text to send: "Company XIV's Little Red Riding Hood show closes Halloween. Want to do the Sunday 6pm with a sitter, or Thursday if you'd rather make a night of it?"
    https://companyxiv.com/tickets/

05. Ahmed Spins brings Afro house to Maspeth  [the guys, music]
    Sat Oct 24 to Sun Oct 25, 11pm to 5am  ·  Knockdown Center, Maspeth  ·  from $38 on Dice
    Johannesburg-raised, and this is the Afro house and amapiano end of the scene rather than a commercial Afrobeats club night with a dress code. Your current listening phase, played properly on a warehouse rig. If the crew wants a two-night weekend, this stacks with Friday.
    Heads up: 11pm to 5am, from about $38 on Dice. That's a genuine all-nighter, so pick your exit time in advance and sort the ride home. It runs until 5am, not 3am.
    Text to send: "Saturday late: Ahmed Spins doing Afro house at Knockdown, doors 11 and it runs till 5. Warehouse, proper sound. Who's in?"
    https://dice.fm/artist/ahmed-spins-rvopy

06. Cars and coffee on Greene Street cobblestones  [guys or date, cars]
    Sat Oct 24, 9am to noon  ·  Greene Street (NYC Car Club x Retromobile NYC), SoHo  ·  free
    This is the Daikoku-register thing, not a dealership lot with a banner. Three blocks of SoHo cobblestone, all makes, organizer rules say no revving and no racing because it's a hang. Nine to noon means you're done before the day starts.
    Heads up: Free, rain or shine, 9am to noon between Broome and Prince. Go early, the good cars leave first and parking down there is its own event.
    Text to send: "Saturday morning there's a proper car meet on the cobblestones in SoHo, 9 to noon, free. Coffee first, then go look at engines?"
    https://www.eventbrite.com/e/cars-coffee-x-retromobile-nyc-tickets-2002352132129

07. Schlingensief's Germany Trilogy, free at Goethe  [the guys, culture]
    Sat Oct 24, 3pm, full trilogy with an introduction  ·  Goethe-Institut New York, Gramercy  ·  free with registration
    Three reunification-era splatter films by the most unhinged German director of the era, screened back to back with an intro from the curator. Free, a hundred seats, and the kind of German programming you keep saying you want and never see listed anywhere. The German talk I had in here sold out, so this is the German slot now.
    Heads up: Saturday 3pm, free but registration is required and seating is first come first served. It's the full trilogy, so block the afternoon.
    Text to send: "Saturday afternoon at the Goethe-Institut they're showing all three of Schlingensief's German reunification horror films, free with an intro. Weird and very German, want in?"
    https://www.goethe.de/ins/us/en/sta/ney/ver.cfm

    self-guided 30-minute and hosted 70-minute sessions  ·  Elahni, Manhattan  ·  $39 for the 30-minute self-guided Reset, $65 for the 70-minute hosted Signature
    Small semi-private Finnish sauna and plunge room in Manhattan, $39 for a 30-minute self-guided round or $65 for a 70-minute hosted one. No scene, no chain energy, and you can actually talk to the person running it. The weeknight version of a full bathhouse day.

ZickZack and the German underground, free  [guys or date, culture]
    gallery hours Thursday–Sunday, 3pm to 8pm  ·  Various/Artists, Gramercy  ·  free
    The Alfred Hilsberg show is at Various/Artists, not the Goethe building, and it's free Thursday through Sunday, 3pm to 8pm. The label that effectively invented the German post-punk underground, laid out in a record shop gallery. Twenty minutes of your time before dinner in Manhattan.
    Heads up: The exhibition is at Various/Artists, 19 Essex St on the Lower East Side, open Thu–Sun 3–8pm and closing Nov 7 — not at the Goethe-Institut.
    https://www.goethe.de/ins/us/en/sta/ney.html
