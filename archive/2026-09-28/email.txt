    Fri Oct 2 to Sun Oct 4, Friday runs as one session, 5pm to 10pm  ·  Franklin Point (Zum Schneider Oktoberfest), Greenpoint  ·  general seating $25 ($35 Saturdays), reserved $35 ($45 Saturdays), VIP table $110, rooftop biergarten $15

    Heads up: Saturday pricing: $35 general seating, $45 reserved, $110 for a VIP table, $15 if you only want the rooftop biergarten. Sessions are ticketed in advance, so confirm the Saturday session time when you buy for four. Friday Oct 2 is a single 5pm-10pm session; the early/late split he wants is Saturday Oct 3 (12-4:45pm and 5:15-10pm).
    Text to send: "Saturday is German Unity Day and Zum Schneider's Oktoberfest closes that weekend in Greenpoint. Getting tickets for the afternoon session, liters and a brass band, you in?"
