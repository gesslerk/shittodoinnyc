============================================================

Halloween season has officially taken over the calendar, which means the weird stuff is finally outnumbering the normal stuff. This week you get 120 cars in a Navy Yard warehouse, a live Rocky Horror with drag and aerial work, and a 60-seat Arabic music premiere nobody you know has heard of. Pick two and you've had a good week.

01. 120 cars in a Navy Yard warehouse  [guys or date, cars]
    Sat Oct 17, doors noon, runs through the afternoon  ·  Agger Fish Warehouse, Brooklyn Navy Yard  ·  from about $50 on Dice
    This is the Daikoku-style thing you said you wanted more of, and it's a ten-minute walk from your door. Sixty thousand square feet of old Navy Yard warehouse, 120-plus classics and JDM builds, music and food, no corny cruise-in energy. Flat floor, daytime, so the kid rides along in the carrier and stares at engines if you'd rather do it as a family thing than a guys thing.
    Heads up: Tickets from about $50 on Dice, doors at noon. Buy before Saturday rather than at the door, and go early if you want to actually hear yourself talk near the cars.
    Text to send: "Saturday afternoon there's a 120-car show in a warehouse at the Navy Yard, ten minutes from me. Come through around noon and we'll walk over."

03. Razor-N-Tape album night at Public Records  [the guys, music]

    Heads up: $27, doors 8pm. Set times weren't posted, so check the venue page the day of if you want to time your arrival. Date corrected: this is Thursday Oct 15, this week — not Oct 23.
    Text to send: "Thursday there's a Razor-N-Tape release party at Public Records, 150-cap room with the big sound system. Doors at 8, tickets are cheap. You in?"

04. New Arabic maqam compositions, 60-seat back room  [the guys, music]
    Wed Oct 14, 7pm  ·  Barbès, Park Slope  ·  cover collected at the door; amount not listed on the calendar
    Five serious players, brand-new instrumental pieces written in Arabic maqam, in the Barbès back room that holds maybe sixty people. Ney, qanun, oud, violin, percussion, no marketing budget, community-run. This is the exact thing you mean when you say you want the scene you had to know someone to hear about. Yes, it's a Wednesday. It's a one-night thing and it's worth breaking the rule.
    Heads up: 7pm, cover collected at the door and the amount isn't posted. Bring cash, show up 20 minutes early or you'll be standing behind the bar crowd.
    Text to send: "Wednesday at 7 there's a one-off night of new Arabic maqam compositions in the back room at Barbes, sixty seats. Early enough to still be a normal weeknight. In?"
    https://www.barbesbrooklyn.com/events

05. Gethard works out an hour at Union Hall  [the guys, comedy]
    Sat Oct 17, 7:30pm  ·  Union Hall, Park Slope

Gethard and Dave Ross are banging on festival material, so it's the raw version, not the polished one. 7:30 start means drinks after and you're still home at a dad hour.
    Heads up: Small room and a name attached, so it will sell out. Buy now, not Saturday afternoon. Bocce and a bar upstairs for after.
    Text to send: "Gethard is working out new material in the Union Hall basement Saturday at 7:30, 150 seats. Grabbing tickets tonight, you want one?"

06. Reggae Rewind takes Littlefield till 4am  [the guys, music]
    Sat Oct 17 to Sun Oct 18, 11pm to 4am  ·  littlefield, Gowanus

    Heads up: 11pm doors, runs to 4am. This is the late one of the week, so don't stack it on top of an early Saturday with the kid.
    Text to send: "Saturday night there's a reggae and dancehall party at Littlefield that runs till 4. Doesn't get going till midnight, so nap first and meet me there at 11:30."

07. Open House New York unlocks 300 buildings  [guys or date, culture]
    Fri Oct 16 to Sun Oct 18, roughly 10am to 6pm Friday through Sunday, varies by site  ·  Open House New York (300+ sites citywide), Citywide  ·  free at most sites; some reservation-only tours are ticketed
    One weekend a year the city opens the doors it normally keeps shut: power plants, water infrastructure, private houses, workshops. Most of it is free and walk-up, and a lot of the good Brooklyn sites are a bike ride from Fort Greene. Build a loop with one friend, or do a couple of stops with the stroller in the morning.
    Heads up: The best sites are reservation-only and those went live October 2, so assume the marquee tours are gone. Walk-up sites are the play. Check the map Friday morning and plan a route instead of wandering.
    Text to send: "Open House New York is this weekend, a few hundred buildings open that are normally locked. Want to bike a Brooklyn loop Saturday morning and hit four or five?"
    https://ohny.org/activity/2026-ohny-weekend
